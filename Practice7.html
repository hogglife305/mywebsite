Fire Evacuation Plan

Fires in buildings are dangerous, as you may only have minutes or seconds to get out safely. Concerns such as smoke inhalation and possible structural collapse are very real depending on the severity of the fire.

High-Rise / 5-Story Building or Higher

  • Know the location of the nearest fire exit.
  • Know the location of the nearest staircase.
  • Follow the arrows on red "Exit" signs.
  • Designate a safe area to meet with co-workers.

If smoke accumulates where you are, stay as low to the ground as possible and move toward a safe room or exit where there is less or no smoke. Avoid breathing smoke whenever possible.
